# BigSteve Labs Brand Guide

## Brand Direction

BigSteve Labs uses a dark technology aesthetic built around clarity, practical engineering, and long-term trust. The visual system combines black and deep navy surfaces, electric blue accents, and silver-white typography.

## Logo Usage

- Use `logo-full.svg` as the primary brand signature.
- Use `logo-icon.svg` where a compact square mark is required.
- Use `favicon.svg` at very small sizes.
- Use `logo-monochrome.svg` for single-color production and light backgrounds.
- Keep the BS monogram upright and unaltered.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, recolor individual letters, or add effects.
- Do not place the logo over busy imagery or low-contrast backgrounds.

## Typography Recommendations

- Primary: Segoe UI, Inter, Arial, or a modern system sans-serif.
- Display weight: 700–800 for the BIGSTEVE LABS wordmark.
- Body weight: 400–500 for descriptive copy.
- Labels and tagline: 600 with moderate letter spacing.
- Use uppercase sparingly for brand names, navigation labels, and short technical categories.
- Do not download or bundle a font solely for the brand assets; prefer system fonts.

## Spacing Guidance

- Keep clear space around the full logo equal to at least the height of the letter `B` in the monogram.
- Keep clear space around the icon equal to at least 12.5% of the icon width.
- Do not display `logo-icon.svg` below 32 × 32 pixels.
- Do not display `logo-full.svg` below 240 pixels wide.
- Preserve the original SVG aspect ratio in every placement.

## Dark and Light Background Use

- On black, obsidian, or deep navy backgrounds, use `logo-full.svg` or `logo-icon.svg`.
- On white or very light neutral backgrounds, use `logo-monochrome.svg`.
- Maintain strong contrast and avoid mid-blue backgrounds that compete with the electric blue accent.
- When a one-color white mark is required on a dark background, render the monochrome artwork in white through the destination design system.

## Tagline Usage

The approved tagline is:

**BUILD • AUTOMATE • INNOVATE**

- Keep the words uppercase.
- Preserve the centered bullet separators.
- Use the tagline as a supporting line, never as a replacement for the BigSteve Labs name.
- Maintain enough space for the tagline to remain legible.
- Do not add punctuation or alternate wording.
